## Which system size do I need
A simple rule: every 1 kW of solar produces about 4 units (kWh) of electricity per day in North India, around 120 units per month. A home with a monthly bill of about 2,000 rupees usually needs 2 to 3 kW. A bill of 4,000 to 5,000 rupees usually needs 5 kW. Shops and small businesses with bills above 10,000 rupees usually need 10 kW or more. Each 1 kW needs about 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free roof.

## Hybrid systems with battery backup
For areas with frequent power cuts we offer hybrid systems that keep essential loads running during outages. A 5 kW hybrid with a 5 kWh lithium battery costs about 1,30,000 rupees more than the on-grid 5 kW system.

## Financing and EMI
We partner with banks and NBFCs for solar loans. EMI options are available for 12 to 60 months, subject to the lender's approval. Example: for a 5 kW system, EMI starts from around 6,500 rupees per month for 60 months, which is often close to the electricity bill it replaces. A 10 percent down payment is usually required.

## Payback and savings
Most home customers recover their investment in 4 to 6 years. Solar panels last 25 years, so a typical home saves several lakh rupees over the life of the system. Exact savings depend on usage, tariff and roof.
