# Frequently Asked Questions and Objection Handling
(Fictional sample data for the demo.)

## Government subsidy
Residential rooftop solar may be eligible for central and state government subsidies. Subsidy amounts and rules change, so we do not promise a fixed amount on the phone. Our team checks current eligibility during the site survey and handles the subsidy application paperwork for the customer at no extra charge.

## Warranty
Solar panels: 25-year performance warranty from the manufacturer. Inverter: 5 to 10 years depending on the model. SunRise Solar gives a 5-year workmanship warranty on installation, wiring and structure.

## Maintenance
Panels should be cleaned with plain water every 2 to 3 weeks, more often in dusty months. We offer an annual maintenance plan with 4 professional cleanings and a health check. The first year of maintenance is free.

## Does solar work during power cuts
A standard on-grid system shuts down during a power cut for the safety of line workers. If power cuts are frequent, a hybrid system with battery backup is recommended.

## Does it work in winter, fog or monsoon
Yes, but generation drops on cloudy or foggy days. Annual savings estimates already account for seasonal variation.

## Roof requirements
A flat concrete roof is ideal. Sloped and tin-shed roofs are also possible with special mounting. The roof should be mostly shadow-free between 9 AM and 4 PM. The engineer confirms this during the free site survey.

## Objection: it is too expensive
Explain that solar is an investment that pays back in 4 to 6 years and lasts 25 years. Offer the EMI option, which can be close to the current electricity bill. Suggest the free site survey to get an exact price instead of a guess.

## Objection: I need to discuss with my family
That is completely fine. Offer to book the free site survey at a time when the family decision maker can be present, so everyone hears the details from the engineer.

## Objection: I will think about it later
Respect the decision. Mention that the site survey is free and without obligation, and offer to call back at a convenient time.

## Objection: I rent my house
Rooftop solar needs the owner's permission. Suggest involving the owner, or consider a system only if the lease is long term.
