## About SunRise Solar
SunRise Solar installs rooftop solar power systems for homes, shops and small businesses in Delhi NCR and Uttar Pradesh (Noida, Ghaziabad, Lucknow, Kanpur, Agra, Meerut). We have completed more than 1,200 rooftop installations since 2019. Our engineers are certified and every installation follows government grid-connection norms.

## How the process works
1. Free site survey: an engineer visits, checks the roof, shading, electricity bill and wiring. Takes about 45 minutes. No charge and no obligation.
2. Proposal: within 2 days you receive a system design, exact price and savings estimate.
3. Paperwork: we handle the net-metering application with the electricity distribution company and help with subsidy paperwork.
4. Installation: usually completed in 2 to 3 days after material delivery.
5. Commissioning: the net meter is installed by the electricity company, typically 3 to 6 weeks after application.
Total time from booking to running system is usually 4 to 8 weeks.

## Site survey details
The site survey is free. Slots are available Monday to Saturday between 10 AM and 6 PM. The customer or a family member should be present and keep a recent electricity bill ready. The engineer needs access to the roof.

## Contact and service hours
Customer support works Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM. After installation, service requests are answered within 48 hours.
